How We Choose

Most Applicants Do Not Make It

In our most recent cohort, 0.89% of applicants finished training and passed the final evaluation. We let more people into training than will finish, because the filter that decides it is hands-on performance, not a polished résumé.

Screened before training

  • A DISC assessment, weighted toward Steadiness and Conscientiousness — reliability, attention to detail, consistency
  • A Keller Personality Assessment for fit and speed of learning
  • Mock client interviews before anyone is put in front of a client
  • The Essential Toolkit finished before the first client interview — Claude and ChatGPT, Canva, a brand-agnostic CRM, Zapier and Asana

Four weeks, instructor-led

  • Database and CRM work that carries across whatever system you use
  • Contract writing and e-signature workflows in dotloop and DocuSign
  • Identifying business processes and writing them up as standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  • A final evaluation that not everyone passes

And it continues after placement

Every assistant keeps access to the VRA Skills Accelerator — specialty tracks, hands-on challenges, and professional certifications from platforms including Anthropic, Google, IBM, HubSpot, Semrush and PMI. When your business needs a skill your assistant does not have yet, they go and get it. We commit to 90% proficiency in your systems within 5 business days of starting with you.

What You Can Hold Us To

The Part That Matters After You Sign

Pricing that does not move

  • $1,500 a month full-time, 40 hours a week
  • $800 a month part-time, 20 hours a week
  • No hourly billing and no upcharges
  • See the full pricing page →

We check in, on a schedule

  • Structured check-ins at 14, 30, 60 and 90 days
  • If the fit is wrong, we swap in a different assistant at no extra cost
  • No trial window to miss and no fine print on the swap

How we handle your business

  • Every assistant signs a confidentiality agreement, and we will sign yours
  • Access is provisioned by you, scoped to the work, and revocable at any time
  • Assistants administer the back office under your direction — you still run the business and supervise your people
  • Anything requiring a professional license stays with your licensed staff
Our Belief System

God, Family, Business

In that order. A strong personal foundation is what makes the work worth doing, and it shows in how we treat a client relationship. The five things we ask of ourselves: integrity, excellence in service, proactivity, resourcefulness, accountability.

A portion of the proceeds from every placement goes to St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, because giving back is part of how we do business.
