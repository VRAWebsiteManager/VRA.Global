What Your VA Can Do

Virtual Assistant Tasks

Every VRA assistant is trained to support the day-to-day of your business, whatever industry you're in.

Administrative Work

  • Schedule important meetings and set appointments
  • Data entry
  • Organize your daily to-do list and weekly calendar
  • Respond to customer emails
  • Gather customer reviews
  • Open house preparation, promotion & management
  • Real estate — pre-listing docs, buyer updates, CRM/database management
  • Restaurants — reservations, vendor coordination, online ordering, menu updates
  • Business operations — invoicing, reporting, client onboarding, vendor lists
  • Custom workflows tailored to your industry

Email Management

  • Weed out any spam
  • Answer inquiries, general questions and follow-ups
  • Organize and filter your inbox
  • Carry out email marketing campaigns and manage email lists

Social Media Management

  • Create content to share on your business pages
  • Schedule daily posts
  • Engage with your followers and groups
  • Analyze weekly statistics and reports for the next marketing strategy

Website Management

  • Develop and update your website
  • Update site content, images, and listings
  • Coordinate with designers/developers on site changes
  • Monitor site performance and fix broken links
Pricing at a Glance

Transparent, Flat-Rate Pricing

No matter your industry, pricing doesn't change — you're paying for dedicated hours, not a per-task menu.

$1,500/month
Full-Time VA
  • 40 hours/week
  • 1-year agreement
  • Invoiced on the 20th of each month
Best for: ongoing admin, CRM management, full inbox/calendar ownership, real estate transaction coordination, or any business that needs a consistent full-day presence.
$800/month
Part-Time VA
  • 20 hours/week
  • 6-month agreement
  • Invoiced on the 20th of each month
Best for: easing into delegation, seasonal support, or lighter task loads like social media, email triage, or appointment setting.
What's included in both tiers: Your VA's full dedicated time, hands-on matching & onboarding to get you paired with the right person from day one, and ongoing VRA support with check-ins at 14/30/60/90 days — no hidden fees, ever, and everything is walked through upfront in your free consultation. If your VA is not the right fit, you can swap in a different VA at any point, at no extra cost — we're focused on finding you a long-term teammate, not just filling a seat. Need to adjust later? Revisit hours and scope at renewal (end of your 1-year full-time or 6-month part-time term), or switch between full-time and part-time as your VA's availability allows.
