Why VRA

Trained Virtual Assistants for Growing Businesses

VRA was founded to give business owners across every industry trained virtual assistance — with a hassle-free process from search to onboarding.

✓

Screened, Then Trained

Every assistant comes all the way through our screening and mandatory 4-week training program — and in our most recent cohort, just 0.89% of applicants made it through training and a final evaluation. We give more candidates a shot at training than will make it, because the filter that matters is hands-on performance and a final evaluation, not a polished resume. The instructor-led program covers real estate database management, contract writing, e-signature workflows in dotloop and DocuSign, and website management; trainees also build their own professional portfolio website, practice in mock client interviews, and complete mindset training before they ever touch client work. Every VA builds in-depth, brand-agnostic CRM knowledge that carries over to whatever system you use, plus deep hands-on expertise in KW Command — which is why we commit to 90% proficiency in your systems within 5 business days of starting with you. Beyond onboarding, every VA has ongoing access to our VRA Skills Accelerator, with nearly 20 specialty tracks, hundreds of hands-on challenges, and 200+ professional certifications available from industry-respected platforms like Anthropic, Google, IBM, HubSpot, Semrush, and PMI — creating rapid learners who quickly adapt to your tools and workflow.

✓

Matched to Your Business

Matching starts with your free Needs Assessment — a two-minute breakdown of where you actually need help. From there, every VA is screened with a DISC Assessment (we look for high Steadiness and Conscientiousness — reliability, attention to detail, consistency) and a Keller Personality Assessment for fit and fast-learning ability. You get matched to how your business actually runs, not just the next available assistant. If your clients speak Spanish or Portuguese, we can match you with a Spanish–English or Portuguese–English bilingual assistant.

✓

Business Support

Support continues after your VA starts, with check-ins at 14, 30, 60, and 90 days to make sure things are on track. If your VA needs to build a new skill for your business, they have access to the VRA Skills Accelerator's library of professional certifications. And if the fit still isn't right, swap in a different VA at any point, at no extra cost — no trial window, no fine print.

✓

Built Differently

Most VA agencies lock you into tiered pricing, time-boxed trial guarantees, or a generic assistant with no industry training. VRA doesn't — flat-rate pricing with no upcharges, an open-ended VA-swap guarantee instead of a one-week trial, and every VA trained specifically for your industry before they ever touch client work.

What Your VA Can Do

Virtual Assistant Tasks

Every VRA assistant is trained to support the day-to-day of your business, whatever industry you're in.

Administrative Work

  • Schedule important meetings and set appointments
  • Data entry
  • Organize your daily to-do list and weekly calendar
  • Respond to customer emails
  • Gather customer reviews
  • Coordinate events, classes & training sessions — logistics, scheduling, materials prep

Email Management

  • Weed out any spam
  • Answer inquiries, general questions and follow-ups
  • Organize and filter your inbox
  • Carry out email marketing campaigns and manage email lists

Social Media Management

  • Create content to share on your business pages
  • Video content production — scripting, filming & editing for reels and posts
  • Schedule daily posts
  • Engage with your followers and groups
  • Analyze weekly statistics and reports for the next marketing strategy
  • See our full Digital Marketing VA page →

Website Management

  • Develop and update your website
  • Update site content, images, and listings
  • Coordinate with designers/developers on site changes
  • Monitor site performance and fix broken links

Lead Management

  • Follow up with new leads and inquiries
  • CRM/database entry, tagging & organization
  • Schedule appointments & showings
  • Nurture campaigns & drip follow-ups

Customer Service

  • Respond to customer inquiries & requests
  • Day-to-day client communication
  • Track and follow up on open items
  • Gather & respond to customer reviews

AI-Enabled Support

  • VRA Skills Accelerator-certified in AI tools (Anthropic, OpenAI Academy, and more)
  • Draft & research using AI assistants
  • Automate reporting & repetitive admin work
  • Build AI-supported workflows for your business

Graphic Design

  • Canva-certified design support
  • Social media graphics & branded templates
  • Listing flyers & marketing materials
  • Consistent, on-brand visuals across platforms
  • See our full Digital Marketing VA page →

Billing & Receivables

  • Prepare and send invoices from your system
  • Call on your past-due invoices and work your aging report
  • Log promise-to-pay notes and escalate what needs you
  • Prepare bids, quotes and proposals from your templates
  • Follow up on outstanding quotes until you get an answer

Process Documentation

  • Record how the work is actually done, exceptions included
  • Write it up as standard operating procedures (SOPs) the business owns
  • Build the checklists and templates your team follows
  • Keep them updated as the process changes — an SOP is a living document
