FAQ

Questions About Bilingual Assistants

Availability, pricing, languages, and where the line is.

What does a bilingual virtual assistant do?

Everything a VRA assistant does, in two languages. Answering and returning calls from Spanish-speaking clients, writing and replying to emails and texts in either language, collecting documents from people who would rather not do it in English, taking inbound inquiries, scheduling, CRM and database upkeep, and translating listings, flyers and social posts so your marketing reaches both audiences. The language is the part everyone notices; the admin underneath it is the same standard as every other assistant we place.

Do you have Spanish-speaking assistants available now?

For most work, yes. We have bilingual assistants ready to place today who have completed our Essential Toolkit training and bring strong administrative and client-service experience from previous roles. They can start on general admin, marketing, client communication and customer support straight away. The one exception is transaction coordination — see below.

Can a bilingual assistant handle transaction coordination?

That one has a wait. Transaction coordination needs our contract training on top of the language, and that comes from a graduating cohort rather than from prior experience. If your Spanish-speaking support needs to run files from contract to close, join the waitlist and we will match you from the next cohort. If what you need is client communication, admin and marketing in both languages, we can start now.

Does a bilingual assistant cost more?

No. $1,500 a month full-time and $800 part-time, the same as any other VRA assistant. There is no upcharge for bilingual or specialized talent, subject to applicable agreement terms. Plenty of agencies price bilingual as a premium tier. We do not, because the assistant is not doing a harder job — they are doing the same job for more of your clients.

Do you place Portuguese-speaking assistants?

Spanish–English is what we place today, and Portuguese–English is on the way. Tell us during the Needs Assessment what your timing looks like and we will tell you exactly where we are with it.

Which industries do your bilingual assistants support?

The same ones as the rest of the bench. Real estate agents and teams with Spanish-speaking buyers and sellers, title and escrow offices, mortgage and lending offices, restaurants and hospitality, home service businesses coordinating Spanish-speaking crews, and any small business whose customers are more comfortable in Spanish than in English.

Is the assistant dedicated to us?

Yes. You are matched with one assistant who learns your business and your clients, rather than a rotating pool or a shared call queue. Matching starts with a two-minute Needs Assessment, and every assistant is screened with a DISC Assessment and a Keller Personality Assessment for fit and fast-learning ability.

How quickly can an assistant start?

Matching typically takes about a week to put candidates in front of you, and once you select your assistant they can realistically start within 24 hours. Both full-time and part-time run on a three-month commitment.

What stays with your team?

VRA virtual assistants provide administrative, marketing, and customer support under the direction of the client. They do not provide legal, financial, or other advice requiring professional licensure, and they are not interpreters for legal or medical matters. Access and permissions are determined by you and are subject to your own security, confidentiality, and compliance procedures.
