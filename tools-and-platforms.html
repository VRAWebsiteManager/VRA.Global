In Client Work Today

Where Our Assistants Work Every Day

Not a wish list. These are the platforms VRA assistants are in for real clients right now, and what they do in them.

Real Estate Transactions

  • KW Command — contacts, tasks, SmartPlans, opportunities, document upload and submission, commission requests, and listing presentation templates.
  • Dotloop and Lone Wolf Transactions — transaction files, document routing, and deadline tracking.
  • HAR / MLS — listing entry and updates, comparable property research, and CMA support.
  • DocuSign and e-signature through Command — setup, sending, and follow-up until documents come back signed.

Title & Escrow

  • Qualia — importing title commitments from Marketplace and hyperlinking them into the file, pre-closing file review and checklist support, lender and underwriter document coordination, and administrative updates to Schedule C once the appropriate title professional or underwriter has approved the documents.
  • Other title production systems — the closing workflow is the same in any platform, so an assistant trained in Qualia ramps onto yours, and we commit to 90% proficiency in your systems within 5 business days of starting with you.

Market Center Back Office

  • KW Command — CDA creation and upload, commission splits, deductions and mentor fees entered for MCA review, and revisions.
  • AccountEdge — agent onboarding and offboarding in the books, monthly agent billing, and office bill invoicing.
  • AgentsOnly, BrokerKit, BrokerBot, Agent Forum, Listing to Leads — the full agent in-process and out-process lifecycle across every system a market center runs.
  • Courted — recruiting pipeline, scheduled recruits, and daily targeted outreach, with weekly agent-in/agent-out and monthly recruiting reports.
  • Zoho — Mentor Program and Preferred Partner records.

Bookkeeping & Admin Finance

  • QuickBooks — invoicing, expense entry, and keeping records current for the bookkeeper or CPA who owns the work.
  • Invoicing and receivables — recurring invoices, accounts receivable follow-up, 1099 and production report distribution, and monthly reporting in whatever system the office already runs.

Marketing, Design & Content

  • Canva — flyers, postcards, brochures, business cards, signage, recognition posts, and listing presentations.
  • Mailchimp — weekly campaigns, distribution lists, and event and open house emails.
  • Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube — scheduling, publishing, engagement, and content calendars.
  • Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Google Business Profile, TubeBuddy — campaign support and performance reporting under your direction.
  • Wix, WordPress — site updates, landing pages, and content publishing.
  • Video production — consultations, scripting, filming coordination, editing, and file management, run as a standing service.

Productivity & Communication

  • Google Workspace and Microsoft Office — inbox and calendar management, shared drives, documents, spreadsheets and presentations.
  • Slack, Zoom — the assistant works where your team already talks, and picks up task requests there.
  • Dialpad, WhatsApp — calls, texts and voice messages, so your assistant is reachable the same way the rest of your team is.
  • Asana, Trello — task boards, recurring workflows, and status tracking.
  • Calendly — scheduling links, appointment coordination, and reminders.
  • Zapier — connecting the tools you already use so handoffs stop being manual.
  • SignUpGenius, Evite, OptiSigns, Linktree — event registration, office signage, and link management.

AI-Supported Work

  • Claude, ChatGPT — drafting and research, summarizing long documents, and speeding up reporting and repetitive admin work.
  • Grok Bot — an AI agent that works inside your own account, directed by your assistant: overnight inbox triage, deadline and closing-calendar tracking, meeting prep, and follow-up drafts waiting for review when your day starts. It prepares work; your assistant decides what goes out.
  • AI is a tool our assistants use under your direction, not a substitute for the person you hired.

What If Your Software Is Not on This List?

90% Proficiency Within 5 Business Days

Most offices run at least one platform nobody outside the company has heard of. That is normal, and it is not a reason to spend three months training someone. We commit to 90% proficiency in your systems within 5 business days of starting with you.

The reason it works is that the skill underneath is the workflow, not the buttons. An assistant who has run transaction files, closing checklists or an accounts receivable process already knows what the software is for before they learn where it keeps things.

How access works

Your assistant works inside the accounts and permissions you provision, under your own security and compliance procedures, and you can revoke that access at any time. VRA verifies each assistant's identity as part of onboarding, and we will sign your NDA on request.
