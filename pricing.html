Transparent, Flat-Rate Pricing

No matter your industry or the skills your VA specializes in, pricing doesn't change — no tiers, no upcharges, just one flat rate for dedicated hours, not a per-task menu.

Part-Time VA
  • 20 hours/week
  • 6-month agreement
  • Invoiced on the 20th of each month
Best for: easing into delegation, seasonal support, or lighter task loads like social media, email triage, or appointment setting.

Revisit hours and scope at renewal (end of your 1-year full-time or 6-month part-time term), or switch between full-time and part-time as your VA's availability allows.
