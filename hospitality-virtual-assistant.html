FAQ

Questions Operators Ask

What a VRA assistant covers across bookings, turnovers, and marketing — including the hours we do and don’t staff.

What does a hospitality virtual assistant do?

A hospitality virtual assistant handles the guest-facing admin and the back office behind it, remotely, so whoever is on the ground stays with the guests. Guest and reservation support: booking inquiries by phone, email and chat, confirmations, reminders, pre-arrival details and change requests, group, event and venue inquiry coordination, turnover scheduling with cleaners and maintenance between stays, listing, rate and availability updates across booking platforms and your own site, and guest follow-up and review requests. Operations and marketing support: vendor and maintenance coordination, ordering and invoice tracking, staff scheduling support, rate and seasonal-offer updates, bookkeeping support in QuickBooks and owner reporting, social media management, and email marketing campaigns.

Do you work with hotels and resorts, or only vacation rentals?

Both, and event venues as well. The work has the same shape wherever guests book: inquiries answered quickly, confirmations and changes handled, listings and rates kept current, turnovers and vendors coordinated, follow-up and review requests sent. VRA assistants are placed with a destination resort and a reservations business today, and the booking and guest-record fundamentals carry from a property management system to a channel manager to a venue calendar.

Guests book around the clock. Can an assistant cover nights and weekends?

Shifts outside standard business hours can be arranged, evenings and overnight included, at the same flat rate with no after-hours upcharge. Weekend coverage can be requested; tell us the days you need and we will tell you what we can fill. Whatever the shift, what an assistant does well is clear the queue fast and keep confirmations, changes, turnovers and follow-up moving, so that work is not landing on whoever is on the ground between guests.

Can an assistant update our listings and rates across booking platforms?

Yes — listing, rate and availability updates across the platforms you sell on and on your own site, along with seasonal-offer updates. Guesty, Hostaway or OwnerRez on the rental side, Cloudbeds or your own property management system for hotels and resorts, and the channels you distribute through, Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com among them. The one you already run is the one your assistant works in, and we commit to 90% proficiency in your systems within 5 business days of starting with you.

How much does a hospitality virtual assistant cost?

$1,500 a month for a full-time assistant at 40 hours a week, and $800 a month for part-time at 20 hours, invoiced on the 20th. It is a flat monthly rate rather than an hourly one, and it does not change by industry or by the skills your assistant specializes in. There is no upcharge for bilingual or specialized talent. The rate covers your assistant’s reserved hours, matching and onboarding, and check-ins at 14, 30, 60 and 90 days, and if the fit is wrong you can swap in a different assistant at any point at no extra cost. Much of the industry quotes an hourly figure, which makes a season hard to budget against.

Can they coordinate cleaners and maintenance between stays?

Yes. Turnover scheduling with cleaners and maintenance, vendor coordination, ordering and invoice tracking are all standard, and are usually the first thing a short-term rental operator hands over.

What stays with our team?

VRA virtual assistants provide administrative, marketing, and customer support under the direction of the client. They do not provide legal, financial, or other advice requiring professional licensure. Access and permissions are determined by you and are subject to your own security, confidentiality, and compliance procedures.

Can a single property afford this?

Yes. Part-time is $800 a month for 20 hours a week and full-time is $1,500 for 40, flat either way. Inquiry response, calendar management, guest messaging and review replies are steady rather than heavy at one property, so twenty hours covers a great deal of it. Increased coverage is available at any time.
