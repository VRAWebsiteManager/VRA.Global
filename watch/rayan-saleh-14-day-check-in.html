Rayan Saleh — 14-day check-in

Two weeks in, Rayan Saleh of The Butcher's Grille doesn't know how he made it this long without an assistant. Juggling many things, and flexible with it.

Transcript

Full transcript of the video above. Speaker names added; wording is as spoken.

Michael Bossart

All right, we are here with Rayan, and Rayan has a virtual assistant. It's the two-week check-in, because we check in at two weeks, 30 days, 60 days and 90 days. Tell us, how is your VA doing for you?

Rayan Saleh

I think she's been doing a phenomenal job. She's made my life so much easier. As I was saying, I don't know how I made it this long without even having a VA. She's been juggling so many things. She's been super flexible, and she's just making my life easier. So I definitely appreciate having her on my team.
