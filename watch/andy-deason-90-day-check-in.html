Andy Deason — 90-day check-in

Ninety days in, Andy Deason's assistant Paul has taken enough off him that he is away from the business more than he has been in years.

Transcript

Full transcript of the video above. Speaker names added; wording is as spoken.

Michael Bossart

We are here today with Andy Deason. Andy, you've had your virtual assistant now for 90 days. Tell me, how is it going?

Andy Deason

I couldn't be happier with my virtual assistant. My virtual assistant's name is Paul. He has learned the ropes faster than probably I — he's exceeding my expectations. He's allowed me to take time off away from the real estate business. I've been able to play golf more in the last three months than I have in the last three years. And he's just been an excellent addition to the team.

Michael Bossart

We love to hear that. Thanks so much, Andy.
