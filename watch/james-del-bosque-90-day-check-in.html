James Del Bosque works in mortgage. Ninety days in, his assistant has taken complete ownership of the inbox and replies to clients and referral partners.

All right, we are here with James. James is in the mortgage world and he has Lawrence, and you've had him now for 90 days. Would you say that he takes ownership of the role that you've given him?

Oh, absolutely. Yeah. Every task that I have given Lawrence, he has been able to take initiative, and he has taken complete ownership of that task, and he has excelled at the tasks that have been given to him.
