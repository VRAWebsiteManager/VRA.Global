Why This Edition Is Different

Written around the line you cannot cross

Lending is the one industry where delegation has a hard edge. An assistant can carry your pipeline, your calendar and your partner communication — and this edition is explicit about where that stops, so nobody has to improvise in the moment.

Fair game

Calendar, CRM and pipeline tracking. Borrower follow-up calls, emails and appointment scheduling. Referral partner communication and the marketing that keeps those relationships warm.

Stays with you

The loan origination system — your assistant holds no login. Underwriting, file setup and condition clearing. Credit reports and borrower financial data. Anything a license is required to do.

The script for when it comes up

A borrower asks about rate on a follow-up call. The guide gives your assistant the actual words to route it to you properly, without sounding evasive or leaving the borrower waiting.

Your compliance officer decides

Every shop draws the line in a slightly different place. The templates are built to be edited, and yours has the final word on where it sits.
