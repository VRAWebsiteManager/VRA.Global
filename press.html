Our founder

Michael Bossart founded Virtual Realty Assistants in 2022, after more than 30 years in real estate, title and financial services. He has worked a real estate transaction from three sides: residential sales as an agent; about a decade in title insurance, which began as a sales rep at Fidelity National Title in Las Vegas and ended as president of Fidelity’s San Antonio division, with two years in between as director of sales at Creditminders, a technology company serving mortgage companies; and brokerage leadership — in 2008 he took over Keller Williams Memorial in Houston, which he still leads. Houston Agent Magazine named him its 2019 Managing Broker of the Year, and Houston newsrooms have gone to him on home buying, disclosure and the Houston market. The coverage below names him.
