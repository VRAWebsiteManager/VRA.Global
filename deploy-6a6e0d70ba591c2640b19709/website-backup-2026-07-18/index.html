What Your VA Can Do

Virtual Assistant Tasks

Every VRA assistant is trained to support the day-to-day of your business, whatever industry you're in.

Administrative Work

  • Schedule important meetings and set appointments
  • Data entry
  • Organize your daily to-do list and weekly calendar
  • Respond to customer emails
  • Gather customer reviews

Email Management

  • Weed out any spam
  • Answer inquiries, general questions and follow-ups
  • Organize and filter your inbox
  • Carry out email marketing campaigns and manage email lists

Industry-Specific Support

  • Real estate — pre-listing docs, open houses, buyer updates, CRM/database management
  • Restaurants — reservations, vendor coordination, online ordering, menu updates
  • Business operations — invoicing, reporting, client onboarding, vendor lists
  • Custom workflows tailored to your industry

Social Media Management

  • Create content to share on your business pages
  • Schedule daily posts
  • Engage with your followers and groups
  • Analyze weekly statistics and reports for the next marketing strategy
  • Develop and update your website
